[1. BRAND FOUNDATIONS & DESIGN DNA]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Vaultory combines ultra-high-net-worth sovereign private banking with high-velocity
decentralized finance. The visual tone is atmospheric, architectural, and razor-sharp:
deep obsidian moss backgrounds, subtle luminous glass reflections, and high-frequency
Volt Lime highlights that guide user focus toward capital velocity and actions.

Core Brand Palette:
- OBSIDIAN MOSS (Primary Base):   #1A1F0F  (Deepest backdrop, infinite canvas)
- DEEP OLIVE (Secondary Surface): #2D3A1A  (Midtone elevation, gradients, borders)
- NEON VOLT LIME (Brand Accent):   #C4FF0D  (Primary CTAs, active pills, yield highlights)
- ELECTRIC GREEN (Gradient Stop): #8BC34A  (Coin glow depth, chart gains, transitions)
- HIGH STARK WHITE:               #FFFFFF  (Display headers, primary text metrics)
- FROST MUTED WHITE:              rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.70) (Body copy, subheaders)
- GLASS HIGHLIGHT BORDER:         rgba(196, 255, 13, 0.25) (Luminous cyber borders)
- AMBIENT RADIAL GLOW:            radial-gradient(circle, rgba(196, 255, 13, 0.15) 0%, transparent 70%)

Surface Material Hierarchy:
Level 0: Deep Obsidian Canvas (#1A1F0F with linear gradient to #2D3A1A)
Level 1: Frosted Obsidian Glass (rgba(26, 31, 15, 0.75) + backdrop-blur-xl + 1px border)
Level 2: Elevated Card Surface (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05) + hover border-[#C4FF0D]/40)
Level 3: Highlight Interactive Active (rgba(196, 255, 13, 0.10) with #C4FF0D glow)

[3. SHADCN/UI & TYPESCRIPT DIRECTORY CONVENTIONS]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard project directory structure:
/src
  /components
    /ui                   <-- REQUIRED: shadcn CLI generated primitives
      button.tsx          <-- Volt-skinned buttons
      dialog.tsx          <-- Frosted glass modal overlays
      slider.tsx          <-- Staking duration & yield slider
      input.tsx           <-- Glass backdrop inputs with #C4FF0D focus rings
      badge.tsx           <-- High-contrast yield and network pills
      tabs.tsx            <-- Segmented control triggers
    /vaultory             <-- Domain-specific composite views
      crypto-hero.tsx     <-- Flagship interactive hero with floating coins
      navbar.tsx          <-- Multi-page brand navigation & wallet trigger
      portfolio-card.tsx  <-- Live balance card with sparkline visualizer
      swap-terminal.tsx   <-- Slippage, quote, and multi-asset DEX widget
      staking-vault.tsx   <-- APY compounding engine & deposit triggers
      token-ticker.tsx    <-- Continuous real-time price tape
      modal-send.tsx      <-- Instant transaction dispatch dialog
      modal-receive.tsx   <-- QR code generator & address copy module
  /lib
    utils.ts              <-- cn() clsx + tailwind-merge helper
    tokens.ts             <-- Standardized asset definitions & mock prices
  /styles
    globals.css           <-- Tailwind directives & ambient radial glow utilities

Why /components/ui is mandatory:
1. Official shadcn CLI ('npx shadcn@latest add ...') writes directly to /components/ui.
2. Isolates headless unstyled primitives (Radix UI) from bespoke domain branding.
3. Enables predictable tree-shaking, multi-developer collaboration, and zero import breakage.
